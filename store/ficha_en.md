## Description
MultiMarket is the shopping list for people who buy at more than one supermarket in Spain. Add what you need and the app suggests where to buy each item based on price, distance or your favorite chains.

LET THE APP PICK THE STORE
• Compares prices across your supermarkets and suggests the cheapest, the closest or your favorite.
• Store-brand products send you to their chain; equivalent items from other brands are recognized for comparison.
• "By item" or "by store" view, always sorted by urgency.

SHOP WITH PEACE OF MIND
• Separates chilled items and runs a cold-chain timer that warns you before it is too late.
• Your cart shows what is bought and what is pending, with the total always in view.
• Detects which store you are in so purchases are logged where you really shop.
• Opens directions in Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze or OpenStreetMap.

BARCODE SCANNER AND COMPARISON
• Scan barcodes to add products and see their details and photo.
• In the store, compare by price per kilo or liter and find cheaper equivalents.

RECEIPTS FROM PDF
• Import your supermarket's digital receipt (PDF or saved image): the app ticks off what you bought, stores prices and learns each chain.

DATED OFFERS
• Note temporary offers and get a reminder before they expire.

SHARED LISTS
• Share your list with family (up to 3 shared lists plus your private one). It syncs instantly and you can tell the others "I'll go".
• Purchase history to repeat a whole shop or re-add a product, plus restock suggestions based on your habits.

PRIVACY AND OPEN DATA
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers. You can delete your account from the app.
• Products come from Open Food Facts, prices from Open Prices and stores from OpenStreetMap. No scraping.
• If you wish, you can contribute product photos to the shared catalog; they are reviewed before publishing and all metadata is removed.
• Open source (MIT): review the code at github.com/8aws/MultiMarket.

Please note: the app interface is currently available in Spanish only and is designed for supermarkets in Spain.

## App Review notes
- No account or password is required: the app creates an anonymous device account on first launch.
- User-generated content: photos and new products go to moderation before becoming public; every photo has a "Report" button, accounts that upload inappropriate content are progressively suspended from sharing, and contact is multimarket@uverse.es.
- Account deletion: Settings → Privacy → Delete my account.
- Location is used on the device only, to show nearby stores and detect the current one.
